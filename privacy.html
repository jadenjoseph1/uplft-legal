UPLFT Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 September 2026

UPLFT is a strength training application. It builds you a session, records how that session went, and uses the result to build the next one. To do that it must hold information about your body and your training. This policy states what it holds, why, who else receives it, and how to have it erased.

1. Who We Are, and How to Reach Us

1.1 The UPLFT application (the "Application") is operated by Jaden Joseph, carrying on business as UPLFT, a sole proprietorship of 2022 Hannington Road, Victoria, British Columbia, Canada ("UPLFT", "we", "us", "our").

1.2 For any question about this policy or about your personal information, contact hello@uplftapp.com. We respond within five (5) business days, and within thirty (30) days to a formal request to access, correct or delete your information.

1.3 If UPLFT is later incorporated, this name and the corresponding name in the Terms of Service are the only two places that change.

2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to the UPLFT iOS application and the backend services it communicates with.

2.2 It does not apply to any other product or service, nor to what a third party does with material you elect to send to them from within the Application.

3. Eligibility

3.1 The Application delivers unsupervised loaded strength training. You must be at least eighteen (18) and no older than eighty (80) to create an account.

3.2 The Application enforces both ends of that range at sign-up, from your date of birth. It is a hard block, not a warning, and there is no means of bypassing it. Above eighty we ask you to train with in-person guidance instead.

3.3 We do not knowingly collect information from any person under eighteen. If we learn that we have, we delete the account and its data. If you believe a minor holds an account, contact hello@uplftapp.com.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Information you provide

  • Email address. Used to identify your account and to send your sign-in code. We never store a password. The Application signs you in with a one-time code, so there is no password to store, leak, or reset.
  • Name and profile photograph, if you choose to provide them.
  • Date of birth, used to verify eligibility under section 3 and to scale what the Application asks of you.
  • Sex, body weight and height, which drive load estimates and calorie targets.
  • Your training, as you record it. Every exercise, set, repetition, load and difficulty rating, with the date, the duration of the session, and whether you completed it.
  • Readiness check-ins. Energy, soreness, which areas are sore, and whether you are unwell. These remain on your device. They shape the session in front of you, are not uploaded, and are therefore not restored if you sign in on another device.
  • Sleep. The hours you slept and your rating of the night. Unlike the rest of the check-in, this is uploaded and restored on a new device, because it forms part of your history rather than one morning's answer.
  • Injuries and limitations. Areas you mark as painful or injured, and movements you block.
  • Food and drink you record, whether typed, spoken, photographed or scanned by barcode, together with the calories and macronutrients attributed to them.
  • Weigh-ins.
  • Progress photographs, if you take them.
  • Your equipment, schedule and goals.
  • Feedback you send us, including any screenshot attached to it.

4.2 Information the Application generates about you

The following is information about you that you did not enter. The Application derives it, and we name it because you would otherwise have no way to know it exists:

  • An assessment of your competence at each movement pattern, and the level assigned to you.
  • Your best recorded performance for each movement, used to select your next load.
  • Estimated one-repetition maxima, inferred from sets you rated as hard.
  • Whether you are stalling, whether you have earned a progression, and whether your capability has decayed following a break.

This information is used to select your next session. It is used for no other purpose.

4.3 Information collected automatically

  • Crash and performance data, when the Application fails or performs poorly. This includes your device model, operating system version, and a technical trace. It is tagged with your account identifier so that we can determine whether one person is encountering the same fault repeatedly.

We operate no product analytics. We do not record which screens you visit or how long you spend on them.

5. Health and Fitness Information

5.1 What we hold

Exercises performed; sets, repetitions, loads and difficulty ratings; session dates, duration and completion; body weight, height and weigh-ins; readiness inputs (sleep, energy, soreness, illness); injuries, pain indicators and movement limitations; food recorded with its calories and macronutrients; progress photographs; and the derived conclusions listed in section 4.2.

5.2 Why we hold it

This information drives the engine. It reads what you lifted, how it went, how long ago, and how you report feeling today, and selects your movements and loads accordingly. Without it the Application cannot build you a programme. That is the entirety of the product.

5.3 What we will never do with it

  • We do not sell your health or training information. Not to any person, at any price, ever.
  • We do not disclose it to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.
  • We do not use it to target advertising, within the Application or anywhere else.
  • We do not disclose it to insurers or employers.

The Application does not read from or write to Apple Health, and holds no HealthKit data.

6. Purposes for Which We Use Your Information

We use the information described in section 4 only for the following purposes:

6.1 to provide the Application to you, including building and adjusting your training programme and calculating your nutrition targets;

6.2 to display your own history and progress back to you;

6.3 to authenticate you and maintain the security of your account;

6.4 to diagnose and correct faults in the Application;

6.5 to administer your subscription;

6.6 to respond to your requests and to communicate with you about the Application;

6.7 to create aggregated and de-identified information as described in section 7;

6.8 to comply with law, and to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim; and

6.9 for any other purpose to which you expressly consent.

7. Aggregated and De-identified Information

7.1 We may create information that is aggregated or de-identified, such that it can no longer reasonably be associated with you or with any identifiable individual, and may retain and use that information without restriction to develop, evaluate and improve the Application and its training engine.

7.2 We will not attempt to re-identify such information, will not permit any third party to do so, and will not publish it in any form from which you could be identified.

7.3 Information that has been de-identified in accordance with this section is no longer personal information, and is not subject to the deletion provisions of section 11.

9. Consent and the Law We Work Under

9.1 Canada. PIPEDA requires your meaningful, informed consent. Because the Application collects health information, that consent must be express: sought specifically, and not inferred from your continued use.

Accordingly, before the Application asks you a single question about your body, before your account exists and before anything is collected, it presents a consent step which names in plain words what health information it will hold and why, links to this policy, and asks you to agree separately from the Terms of Service, by its own checkbox. We record which version of this policy you agreed to, and when. You may withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account, which erases the information the consent covered.

9.2 Quebec. Law 25 applies if you reside in Quebec. It affords you the rights described in section 12, and additionally the right to be informed before your information is used to render a decision about you based exclusively on automated processing. The Application's engine selects your exercises and loads automatically, which is the whole of the product, and section 4.2 names every conclusion it draws. Nothing it decides affects your credit, employment, insurance, or access to any service. On request, we will tell you which of your information was used, the principal reasons underlying the decision, and how to correct anything inaccurate, and you may submit observations to a person who will review the decision.

9.3 United States. Most state privacy statutes apply only above a size threshold that UPLFT does not approach. Washington's My Health My Data Act is the exception: it has no threshold whatsoever, and it governs precisely the category of information the Application holds. It requires a separate policy, and there is one: the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, which must be reachable by its own link rather than buried within this document. Nevada and Connecticut maintain comparable health-data rules, and that policy is drafted to satisfy them.

11. Deleting Your Account

11.1 You may delete your account from within the Application: Settings, Delete account. You need not contact us.

11.2 What is deleted: your profile, your email address, your name, your training history, your readiness inputs, your injury indicators, your food record, your weigh-ins, your progress photographs, your avatar, any feedback you sent us together with its screenshots, every archived earlier version of your data, and the conclusions the engine drew about you. Your account itself is removed, so that you cannot sign in again, and the copy held on your device is erased. If you connected Google Calendar, that permission is revoked at the same time.

11.3 Deletion is all or nothing. If any part of it fails, we delete nothing, leave your account intact, and tell you so, rather than destroying your account and leaving fragments that nothing can afterwards reach. You may simply try again.

11.4 What survives, and why:

  • Payment records, where Canadian tax law requires us to retain them.
  • Crash reports already transmitted to Sentry. These carry your account number and age out on Sentry's own ninety-day schedule. They contain no training data, no photographs and no food record.

That is the entire list. We would rather name those two than claim a clean sweep we do not perform. If you wish the crash reports removed sooner, contact hello@uplftapp.com and we will do so by hand.

11.5 Deletion does not cancel your subscription. Apple administers billing, and you must cancel in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions on your device. We cannot cancel it on your behalf.

12. Your Rights

12.1 You may request a copy of all information we hold about you, request correction of anything inaccurate, and request that we cease using your information. Contact hello@uplftapp.com and we will respond within thirty (30) days, the period prescribed by PIPEDA.

12.2 There is no export function within the Application. Request a copy and we will produce it.

12.3 Quebec residents additionally have the right to receive the information they provided in a structured, commonly used technological format, and to have it communicated to another organisation. Request it and we will produce it.

12.4 Residents of Washington, Nevada and Connecticut hold specific rights over health data in addition to the foregoing, set out in the separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

12.5 Exercising any right under this section is free of charge, and we will never treat you differently for having done so.

13. Your Responsibilities

13.1 Accuracy. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information you provide, including your age, sex, body weight, height, injuries and limitations. The Application's output depends entirely upon it, and we cannot verify it.

13.2 Account security. Access is authenticated by a one-time code sent to your email address. You are responsible for maintaining the security of that address. Any person able to read your email can access your account.

13.3 Content of others. You must not upload a photograph of any person who has not consented to it.

14. Accountability

14.1 Canadian privacy law requires a named individual to be accountable for the information an organisation holds and for the policies governing it. For UPLFT that individual is Jaden Joseph, contactable at hello@uplftapp.com.

14.2 UPLFT has no employees and no contractors. No other person is accountable, and no other person has access.

15. Breach Notification

15.1 If your information is lost, stolen, or accessed by a person who should not have had access, and there is a real risk of significant harm to you, we will:

  • notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as PIPEDA requires;
  • notify you directly, by email, as soon as we reasonably can;
  • state what occurred, what information was involved, and what you can do about it; and
  • maintain a record of the breach, which we are required to keep for two years and to produce to the Commissioner on request.

15.2 If you are in the United States, the Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule also applies to us. We will notify you without unreasonable delay and within sixty (60) days, notify the Federal Trade Commission, and notify media where that rule requires.

15.3 We will not wait for certainty before telling you. If we know enough to be concerned, that is enough to notify you.

16. Security

  • In transit: all traffic travels over HTTPS.
  • At rest: encrypted by our hosting provider.
  • No passwords: there is no password to steal, because we do not use them.
  • Progress photographs and feedback screenshots are private: they reside in storage that is not publicly readable, and access requires a signed link issued only to you.
  • Your profile photograph is not private. It is stored in a publicly readable bucket so that the Application can display it, which means any person holding or guessing its address can open it without signing in. The address contains your account identifier and is published nowhere, but it is not a secret. Do not select an avatar you would be uncomfortable having public. (Progress photographs are not stored in this manner.)
  • Access: UPLFT is operated by one person, Jaden Joseph, who is the only human being with access to the database.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. What we can state is what we actually do, which is the list above.

17. Device Permissions

Each permission is requested when you first use the feature requiring it, not on launch, and the Application functions without any of them:

  • Camera: to photograph a meal, scan a barcode, or take a progress photograph.
  • Photographs: to select an existing image, and to save a share image.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to allow you to dictate what you ate or trained.
  • Motion: to detect device rotation so that a demonstration can fill the screen.
  • Calendar: to add a booked session to your calendar.
  • Notifications: to remind you. These are scheduled on your device. We hold no push token and cannot send you anything remotely.

18. Sharing to Other Applications

When you share a session image, it is generated on your device and passed to the iOS share sheet. It travels wherever you send it, and from that point is governed by the receiving application's own policy. We never receive a copy, and we do not record what you share.
